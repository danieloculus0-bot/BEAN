BEAN is running on Jetson.

Little name. Big idea. Built for the long game.

The simple version

AI answers questions. BEAN is trying to learn from what happens next.

Most AI assistants are pretty good at answering you right now. But what happens when the answer is wrong? Or you find a better way? Or you come back next week?

BEAN is being built around memory, evidence and feedback so AI can build on earlier work instead of starting from scratch every time.

Without useful history

“Try this fix.” You try it. It fails. Tomorrow the assistant may suggest the very same thing.

What BEAN is working toward

“We tried that already. It didn't work. Here's what we learned, and here's the next idea worth testing.”

Four things that matter

Less starting over. More getting somewhere.

01 / Remember

Keep useful history

Keep track of notes, decisions and results. Some BEAN memories already survive a restart.

02 / Check

Ask, “How do we know?”

Compare claims, look at sources and flag when an answer needs more evidence.

03 / Adjust

Learn from results

In specific tests, BEAN can score outcomes and change how it makes choices. Broader learning is still research.

04 / Work together

Help other AI systems

BEAN is intended to work with language models and tools, not replace everything already doing its job.

Here's an easy way to picture it

Think of a mechanic who keeps good service records.

A mechanic who remembers what you fixed last month is more useful than one who acts like you've never met. That's the kind of continuity BEAN is designed to help AI build.

Say you're troubleshooting a machine...

1. Try an idea.

Pick a possible fix and keep track of why it seemed worth trying.

2. Check the result.

Did it work? Did it make things worse? What evidence do you have?

3. Use the lesson.

Next time, look up what happened instead of guessing all over again.

Built and running

BEAN has a working local AI Core.

On the Jetson, BEAN has completed real language reasoning with local Qwen2.5, recovered its persistent memory, and recorded a retrievable result. That is the current working foundation.

November 1, 2026 milestone: BEAN Core v0.13.1 passed its on-device startup checks and used CUDA-accelerated Qwen2.5 to complete a live reasoning request. Face and camera have worked separately; voice-to-Core and motor integration are next. Technical details and test record.
