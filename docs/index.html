The architecture · Scored memory and neural search

A better decision needs better evidence

Observe. Let the policy act and receive a measurable reward.

Learn. Update tabular Q-values or neural-network weights through actual training.

Evaluate. Compare against conventional learners on separate validation games.

Remember. Score the evidence, compress weights, then file their SHA-256 identity and journal receipt.

Recheck. Reject unreliable offspring and test adopted policies on a sealed holdout.

SHA-256 verifies recorded bytes, not whether the underlying observation was true. Neural evolution is experimental and has not demonstrated consistently superior learning.

A preregistered-style two-cohort validation gate rejected the evolved candidate on seed 44 before sealed holdout testing. The evolved policy still underperformed standard DQN by 4.85 bricks on that seed. Selected policies on seeds 45 and 46 showed gains of 0.23 and 1.90. Across all three seeds, the raw evolved policy averaged 0.91 fewer bricks than standard DQN. This is not a replicated evolutionary advantage; adoption-gate results reflect fallbacks and should not be confused with better neural training.

Controlled synthetic game, engineered three-number observations, not DeepMind pixel Atari. Draft research PR #107 only, with no production deployment or replicated advantage. The complete October 12 findings and evidence limits are recorded in the Main README learning section.

Neuroevolution · DQN offspring selectionImprovement not replicated

Neural mutations work; performance is unstable

The first three training seeds produced raw evolved-minus-standard DQN differences of −4.53, −8.63 and +0.82 bricks/game. Fresh gated trials rejected one weak offspring and selected two; the raw evolutionary performance is still inconsistent.

What BEAN learned from failure

Scoring against one validation cohort was insufficient. BEAN's experimental selector now checks two validation cohorts and rejects a policy that fails either. Whether this approach generalizes is still being tested.
