02 / The flow

Utility before speculation.

The intended accounting flow separates customer funds, service costs and actual eligible protocol surplus.

01 · USE

Request work

Customers access an eventual BEAN-connected service with disclosed fees and usage terms.

02 · VERIFY

Record results

Verified outcomes and costs become auditable receipts, rather than unverifiable performance claims.

03 · RECONCILE

Cover obligations

Operating costs, liabilities and customer-owned balances are excluded before calculating distributable surplus.

04 · ALLOCATE

Split 50 / 50

Under the proposed policy, half of eligible BNRVT surplus is burned and half enters a transparent community treasury.

06 / Questions

Know what this is.
And what it isn't.

Can I buy BeanRivet right now?

No. There is no issued BNRVT token, sale, presale, accepted wallet, or investment offering associated with this site.

Will the community receive 50% of profits?

The proposal is to allocate 50% of eligible protocol surplus token units to a community treasury, subject to documented spending rules. It is not a promise of cash distributions, passive yield or tokenholder profit sharing.

Where do unused customer credits go?

They remain customer balances, subject to clearly disclosed service terms. The proposed surplus calculation does not silently capture unused prepaid balances. Any round-up or fee would require advance disclosure and consent.

Does burning tokens automatically make them more valuable?

No. A burn can reduce supply, but token value depends on many factors including actual utility, demand, liquidity, issuance, market conditions and risk.

Does BEAN or AIscend already run this network?

No. BEAN has experimental continuity and learning infrastructure; AIscend has experimental crypto strategy software. BeanRivet's network accounting, treasury and token contracts are proposed future work.

Is the ticker or name legally cleared?

No. BNRVT and BeanRivet are working identifiers. Trademarks, relevant market conflicts, domains and regulatory obligations must be confirmed before a commercial launch.
