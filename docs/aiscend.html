Unattended research supervisor

The locally running research supervisor checks newly completed hourly bars, schedules research, and preserves progress across restarts. It does not execute wallet orders or continue when the desktop server stops.

Independent forward testing and BUY gate

A separate research module evaluates forward-only five-minute candles without placing orders. The BEAN policy gate can only confirm or veto BUY decisions already proposed by the trading engine.
