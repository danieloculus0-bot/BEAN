Control comparison: even an untrained agent with the same loop-breaker solved 232/240 maps. The largest evidence for learned Q-values is the reduction in navigation cost, not the nearly perfect success rate by itself.
What is actually measured and what isn't?

The policy saw 3,000 training maps. An independent 180-map validation split selected among 18 episodic-escape settings. The 240-map test split used different deterministic random seeds. The holdout cases were not used to update the policy or choose evaluation settings. The task still supplies legal moves, target-relative features, and shaped rewards. It's strong transfer to novel layouts within the same puzzle type, not yet cross-domain reasoning or self-directed real-world autonomy.
