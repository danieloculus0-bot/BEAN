Meet BEAN · Experimental AI agent

AI that remembers. AI that learns.

BEAN is an AI agent designed to give artificial intelligence continuity, experience, judgment and a way to improve its decisions. It keeps useful lessons, considers available evidence, and uses feedback to make a better choice next time. BEAN can work with different language models without depending on a single model's memory.

It is real experimental Python software with working memory, reasoning and learning components. Its broader independence and ability to learn across many unrelated tasks are still being developed.

What can BEAN do?

Remember

Keep past experiences, useful lessons and decision records across sessions, even when the AI model changes.

Think before acting

Compare what it knows with new information, reuse an established answer and recognize when more help is needed.

Learn from results

Record mistakes and successes, adjust limited learned strategies and test whether those changes actually help.

Show its work

Keep inspectable tests, sources and failed experiments so progress can be checked rather than assumed.

Who is BEAN for?

Researchers studying persistent reasoning or agent learning; developers building AI assistants that need recoverable memory and evidence; teams exploring model-agnostic decision support; and organizations interested in evaluating bounded integrations or prototypes.

What is not yet established?

BEAN has not demonstrated frontier-model-level standalone reasoning, general-purpose independent transfer, assured autonomous reliability, or consistently superior performance on held-out tasks. Hosted plugin functionality is narrower than the full Python Core. Tested mechanisms and scientific outcomes should be evaluated separately.
