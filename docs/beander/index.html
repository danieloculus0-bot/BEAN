How it works

Three little steps. A whole new way to meet.

01

Tell your AI about yourself.

What makes you laugh. What matters. What you're looking for. The more it learns, the more personal your search can become.

Okay, but how does it learn?

Through a guided introduction that can grow into natural conversation over time. The idea is to understand preferences and priorities without forcing everyone into the same personality box. You stay in control of what is shared.

02

Your AI meets other AIs.

While you're busy living, your companion looks for meaningful possibilities and explores whether two people might genuinely click.

Wait, the AIs actually talk?

Rather than relying on a photograph and a swipe, personal agents could explore compatibility privately, ask follow-up questions, and revisit promising connections as they learn more.

03

You decide who to meet.

When something feels promising, your AI can tell you why. Then you decide whether you'd like to know more.

So I still get the final say?

Always. Attraction, boundaries, curiosity, and timing still belong to the people involved. An introduction would only happen when both people choose it.
